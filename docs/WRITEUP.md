# 3AM: Wall Street keeps hours. You don't.

The US stock market is open from 9:30 in the morning to 4 in the afternoon, New York time, Monday to Friday. That's only 32 and a half hours out of the 168 in a week. For most of the world, the market opens in the evening or the middle of the night, and getting a US brokerage account from outside the US usually means paperwork, minimum deposits, bank wires and days of waiting. 3AM fixes that. It lets anyone buy and sell big US stocks like Nvidia, Tesla and Microsoft at any hour, from a simple app at trade3am.xyz, or just by sending a text on iMessage.

This works because of tokenized stocks: digital tokens that track a real share, issued by bStocks and Ondo on BNB Chain. One Nvidia token follows the price of one Nvidia share, and because it lives on a blockchain it can be traded any time, from anywhere. The problem has always been that using them felt like crypto homework. You needed a wallet, a 12-word seed phrase, special coins to pay network fees, and an exchange you trusted. 3AM hides all of that behind something that feels like an ordinary trading app, while you still own everything yourself. Here's everything it does.

FEATURE 1: SIGN UP IN SECONDS, WITH YOUR OWN WALLET. You log in with Google, email or X, and 3AM quietly creates your own wallet for you through Privy. There's no seed phrase to write down and no exchange account to open. The money in that wallet is yours, not ours. Every trade you make on the site is approved by you, and you pay the tiny network fee yourself. 3AM never holds your funds.

FEATURE 2: DEPOSIT BY CARD. You tap Deposit, choose Deposit with card, pick your currency and amount, and pay with a card or Apple Pay. A licensed payment company, MoonPay, sends USDT (a digital dollar) straight into your own wallet. 3AM never touches the money; it just watches the blockchain until the dollars arrive and tells you when they land. If you already have crypto, you can send USDT directly with a QR code. Trades also need a few cents' worth of BNB to pay the network, so if you have none, 3AM explains why and lets you buy a couple of dollars' worth by card as well. You can go from a bank card to owning Nvidia without ever touching an exchange.

FEATURE 3: BUY AND SELL STOCKS 24/7. You pick a stock, type an amount from one dollar up, and tap Buy. Before you confirm, you see the price, the 1% fee and a guaranteed minimum number of shares you'll receive. Then you watch each step tick off live (pay the fee, buy the stock, done) instead of staring at a spinner. Selling works the same way in reverse: choose 25, 50 or 100 percent and get dollars back in your wallet.

FEATURE 4: LIVE PRICES, CHARTS, NEWS AND YOUR P&L. Every stock page has a live price that updates in real time, a chart you can switch between the last hour, day, week or all time, the latest news about the company, and a "Your position" card showing exactly how much you hold, the average price you paid, and whether you're up or down. A watchlist and search let you find any stock, basket or person quickly, and the app works in both light and dark mode.

FEATURE 5: HONEST ABOUT MARKET HOURS. At 3am the real stock market is closed, so a token's price can drift away from the real share. 3AM shows the real share's last price, the gap between the two (the "premium"), and a clear "Market closed" label. Nobody mistakes a midnight price for an official one, and nobody gets surprised.

FEATURE 6: JUST TEXT IT, ON IMESSAGE AND TELEGRAM. This is the feature that makes 3AM different. In settings you connect your phone number, send one text to prove it's you, and from then on you can talk to 3AM like a friend. Ask "what's Nvidia doing tonight?" and it replies with the live price and move. Ask "any news on Tesla?" and you get a short summary. Ask "how's my portfolio?" and you get your total, each stock you hold and your profit or loss. Say "put 20 bucks into Nvidia" and it replies "Buy $20 of NVDA? Fee $0.20. Reply YES to buy." You reply YES, it buys, and it tells you exactly what you got. You can also sell by text, for example "sell half my Nvidia". There's no app to open, no chart to read, just a text message.

FEATURE 7: TEXT TRADING THAT CAN'T GO ROGUE. Letting a bot touch your money is a big deal, so this part was built with strict rules. Trading by text is off until you switch it on in the app, and you set your own limits, which start at $50 a buy and $200 a day. Nothing happens until you reply YES, and that YES is checked by plain code, not by the AI, so the AI can never decide to trade on its own. A text can only swap your dollars for a stock or back again inside your own wallet; there's no way to withdraw or send money anywhere by text. Switching it off removes access completely. The AI also isn't allowed to make things up: before any reply goes out, the system checks that every price, amount and link in it came from real data, and if anything doesn't match it sends a safe reply built directly from that data instead. If you never turn trading on, the assistant still answers questions, and "buy" sends you a link that opens the app with the amount already filled in.

FEATURE 8: SOCIAL TRADING. Trading is more fun with other people. The home page shows the week's top trades and a Hall of Fame of the best calls, and live alerts pop up when people make trades, like "@ada bought $50 of NVDA". Every stock has a Holders tab that shows who owns it, the price they got in at and how they're doing, plus a comment section. You can follow traders whose calls you like and see what they buy. All of it is based on real, completed trades, so nobody can fake their results.

FEATURE 9: BASKETS. If you can't pick one stock, you can build a basket of two to five, like "Semiconductor Core" or "AI Cloud". You choose the stocks, drag a bar to set the split (for example 50% Nvidia, 30% AMD, 20% Broadcom), give it a name and launch it. Once launched, the recipe can never be changed, so nobody can swap in different stocks after you've bought. When you buy a basket, 3AM buys each stock for you one by one and locks them into your own position, a personal record on the blockchain of exactly what you own. You can sell a basket back to dollars, or redeem it to take the actual stock tokens out.

FEATURE 10: CREATORS GET PAID, AND HOLDERS GET A PRIVATE CLUB. Whoever creates a basket earns a quarter of the 1% fee every time someone buys it, and can claim those earnings straight from the contract. Creators can also attach a private Telegram group to their basket, which 3AM reveals only to people who actually hold that basket.

HOW IT WORKS UNDER THE HOOD. All prices and trades come from Binance's Web3 API, which gives apps access to tokenized stocks. 3AM uses its stock data to get the full list of available stocks, live prices, the real share's price and whether the US market is open or closed. It uses its market data for the price history behind the charts. And it uses its trading engine to find the best price across exchanges, build each trade and track it until it's finished. Every request to Binance is made securely from our server, so no secret keys ever reach your browser. 3AM is also built to respect Binance's regional rules, so trading is switched off in the countries Binance doesn't serve.

THE SAFETY CHECKS YOU NEVER SEE. A trade only counts as done when Binance confirms it filled and the blockchain shows the shares actually landed in your wallet. Just sending an order isn't enough. If you close the app halfway through, it picks up where it left off without buying twice. Before you sign anything, 3AM compares the offered price with the real market price and refuses the trade if it's more than 5 percent worse, so you're protected from bad prices. All money math is done in exact whole units, never rounded decimals, so not a cent goes missing.

A VAULT WITH NO BACK DOOR. The baskets live in a smart contract on BNB Chain, a public program anyone can inspect on BscScan. It was deliberately built so that even the team running 3AM has no way to take users' stocks or creators' earnings. It can be paused for new activity in an emergency, but withdrawals always keep working. Before any stock is allowed on 3AM, we test it against a copy of the real BNB Chain by sending it into the vault and back out. All 86 stock tokens we tested, 46 from bStocks and 40 from Ondo, came out exactly as they went in.

WHAT IT'S BUILT WITH. The app is built with Next.js on Vercel, Privy for logins and wallets, Supabase for profiles and the social feed, Solidity and Foundry for the vault contract, Binance's Web3 API for stock data and trading, Photon for iMessage and Telegram, and Gemini for the assistant's conversation. It's backed by automated tests for the contract, the fee math, the trading steps and the Binance connection.

FEES AND STOCKS. Buying costs 1%. For a basket, a quarter of that goes to its creator. Selling costs 1% of what you get back. Creating a basket is free, the network fee is a few cents of BNB that goes to the network, not to us, and the minimum buy is one dollar. You can trade big names and ETFs from bStocks and Ondo, including Nvidia, Tesla, Microsoft, Meta, Google, AMD, Broadcom, Micron, Intel, SanDisk, the S&P 500 (SPY), the Nasdaq 100 (QQQ), TQQQ and SOXL, with more as Binance adds them. There's deliberately no leverage, no borrowing and no betting on prices going down. You simply own the stock, and nobody holds your money for you.

WHAT'S NEXT. Price alerts and morning briefings by text, selling baskets by text, more stocks as Binance adds them, and stats for basket creators. The goal stays the same: a place where anyone, anywhere, can own a piece of the world's biggest companies at 3am as easily as sending a text.

Wall Street keeps hours. You don't. This is 3AM.
